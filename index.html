About Noerix

A technology company with a clear focus.

Noerix OÜ is a technology company incorporated in Estonia, building products at the intersection of location intelligence, artificial intelligence, and social discovery.

Our mission is to make spontaneous human connection easier — closing the gap between the digital world and the places where people actually live, work, and meet. We build tools that are fast, intelligent, and grounded in the real world.

Everything we build is designed to scale globally from day one, with privacy-respecting architecture and AI embedded at the core — not bolted on as a feature.
